01About

I completed a PhD in Business Analytics at RMIT, building two-stage stochastic programming for bank balance-sheet optimisation under Basel III, calibrated to real Australian bank data. My research sits at the intersection of quantitative finance and regulation: systemic risk measurement, credit contagion across portfolios, and how capital behaves under stress.

Alongside research I teach quantitative and analytics subjects at RMIT and Monash, and deliver an advanced analytics program through Mentem by UNSW. I am affiliated with the Department of Banking and Finance at Monash Business School. Before academia I spent a decade in industry: credit risk and cost planning at Toyota, then data and AI delivery leadership at the Fair Work Commission.

This site collects the applied work: models you can run, not just papers you can cite.
