Research

Systemic credit risk from public disclosures

Measuring how much credit risk banks share, using realised losses and APRA Pillar 3 disclosures rather than market prices alone. This includes joint default modelling, Shapley allocation of expected shortfall, and comparing these measures with CoVaR and SRISK.

Dependence and tail contagion

Copula models of how losses cluster in bad times, from loan default correlation and credit default swap indices to tail contagion among Australia's systemically important banks.

Bank balance sheets and capital

Two-stage stochastic optimisation of bank balance sheets under Basel III capital and liquidity constraints, with credit contagion between portfolios. Related work develops capital allocation that switches between Value-at-Risk and Expected Shortfall as market regimes change.

Climate risk and banks

How banks' climate adaptation disclosures relate to their physical exposure and credit risk.
